## [Airbnb Snowballs in Vermont Ski Towns, Bringing Cash and Concerns](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/airbnb-snowballs-in-vermont-ski-towns-bringing-cash-and-concerns-10220050/)

### Clarity and accuracy:

* I was easily able to understand the point of this article and what the author was communicating, **I would rate it a 4/5 for clarity.** 
   * The main idea of the article was the benefits and disadvantages that are coming to Vermont as Airbnb gets more popular.
   * I would say the headline did a good job of summing this idea up and giving the reader an idea of what to expect.
* There were two data visualizations in this article. The first was a diagram showing the 5 Vermont towns with the most Airbnb listings. The second visualization was an interactive one where you can see data for each of those 5 towns including number of Airbnb listings, number of hosts, whether the listings are entire homes or single rooms, what percent of housing in the town is listed on Airbnb and more useful information. **I would give the visualization in this article a 5/5**
   * The most efficient way to navigate the visualization was with the interactive functions and hovering over the graphs with the mouse to see the exact numbers.
   * An improvement I would suggest for the interactive portion would be a way to compare data from two different towns.
* Based on my read of the article and my own experience living in Vermont and being in these towns, this seems pretty accurate. I would give it a **4/5**
   * As far as improvements, some quotes aren't very strong and could be backed up with additional data, such as "something like half the homes are vacation homes"
   * The reporter could have also reached out to Airbnb about their thoughts on towns putting their own restrictions on listings. 
   * The article provides all sources used and published their code on github.
   * It definitely seems like everything was explained well, I didn't finish reading it with any questions.

### Quality:

* The quality in which the data was presented was very good. I give it a **5/5**.
   * The visualizations are pleasant on the eyes and easy to understand. The interactive portion is really nice and gives a lot of information.

### Type and color:

* The use of color and type in this piece were pretty good, but not outstanding.
   * Everything looked good together and matched, but there was nothing super impressive. 
  
* Type: **3/5**
   * The fonts used in this don't have anything wrong with them. Nothing is clashing or distracting but nothing is particularly eye-catching either. Simple, readable fonts are best for presenting data in my opinion but for the first graphic maybe the type could have been more creative to catch eyes.
* Color: **4/5**
   * The first visualization in this piece uses color well, using a pleasant blue color theme to match the topic of ski resorts. The interactive visualization isn't super colorful but looks clean and easily comprehensible.
   
### Creativity:

* **I give this piece a 3/5 for creativity**. Aside from the interactive portion, this piece is pretty standard and does not do much out of the ordinary in how it presents the data.
